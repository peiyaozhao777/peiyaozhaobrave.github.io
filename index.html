Research

Two directions, one approach.

2019 – 2023 · Wearable Electronics Laboratory
Thin-film materials & sensing

Hands-on process work: depositing thin functional films by vapor deposition on flexible and textured substrates, then building and testing colorimetric sensors from the film up.

  • Vapor deposition and process-parameter tuning for film quality, uniformity, and adhesion
  • Film and device characterization with SEM, UV-Vis, four-point probe, and contact angle
  • Diagnosing sensor failure modes such as fouling and surface-texture effects
  • Machine learning on large-scale, multi-metric colorimetric sensor output
Same habit in both: measure carefully, find what drives failure, fix it.
2023 – present · NARS Laboratory
Data-driven infrastructure & emissions

Scaling the analysis side: Python and R pipelines, machine learning, and decision tools for emissions and water infrastructure data with millions of records.

  • Data cleaning, validation, and QA/QC for 3M+ record datasets
  • Clustering and typologies (EFA, GMM, latent class models)
  • Explainable ML (XGBoost with SHAP) and time-series forecasting
  • Scenario discovery under uncertainty (PRIM, Latin Hypercube Sampling)

Dissertation: four studies, one framework

01
Transportation emission inventory

Characterizing vehicle emission patterns across 300 MSAs using road network typology and socioeconomic context.

02
Road network typology

Latent-class clustering of MSA road networks with OSMnx variables to reveal structural patterns linked to congestion and crash outcomes.

03
Water violation typology

EFA + GMM clustering of EPA SDWIS data (1991–2023) to identify non-compliant system archetypes and track how systems move between them over time.

04
Scenario discovery

PRIM-based scenario discovery with Latin Hypercube Sampling and SHAP interpretation to inform IIJA infrastructure investment decisions.

The four studies share a decision-making framework that examines the implications of the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act, integrating transportation and water infrastructure research across spatial, temporal, and socioeconomic dimensions.
